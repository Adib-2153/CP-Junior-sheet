# The Junior Sheet, finally usable

**Open it → [juniorcpsheet.netlify.app](https://juniorcpsheet.netlify.app/)**

Every competitive programmer knows the Junior Sheet — a legendary, sprawling roadmap of ~1,200 problems that's supposed to take you from "just learned loops" to "cracking Div2-D." The problem was never the content. It was the spreadsheet. Scrolling through a wall of plain links, losing your place, forgetting what you'd already solved, never knowing if today's problem was actually the right one to be doing.

So this exists instead: the same roadmap, but as something you'd actually want to open every day.

## What you get

- **The full Junior Sheet, in order** — all seven sheets, Div2-A through Div2-D, lesson videos dropped exactly where you're meant to watch them, and every editorial or video solution linked right where you need it.
- **CP-31** — when the full sheet feels like too much, 31 handpicked problems climbing from 800 to 1900 rating. A tight, honest gut-check of where you stand.
- **Beyond Codeforces** — a curated shortlist from AtCoder, CodeChef, and LeetCode, for when one platform starts to feel like an echo chamber.
- **Your Codeforces handle, synced automatically** — drop in your handle once and solved problems check themselves off, pulled live from Codeforces' own public data. No login, no token, nothing handed over.
- **Manual tracking for everything else** — because not every problem you solve lives on Codeforces, and the sheet shouldn't pretend otherwise.
- **Real filtering** — by sheet, by difficulty, by status — so you're never scrolling past 200 rows to find where you left off.

## The quiet part

No backend, no signup, no ads, nothing tracked. It's a single page that runs entirely in your browser — your progress and your handle stay on your machine, and the only network calls it makes are straight to Codeforces' public API. Close the tab, come back next week, and it's exactly where you left it.

Just open the link and start climbing.
